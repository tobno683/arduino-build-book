How every guide is laid out

The price is a real breakdown

Every part is a line item with a unit price, a quantity and links to several suppliers - the cheap slow one and the expensive fast one. Shared stock you buy once (resistor kits, jumper wires) is counted separately so a project's headline price is what that project actually adds to your shelf.

The 3D view and the wiring table cannot disagree

Both are generated from one list of connections. Every board in the model is at its real size with its real pin positions, so when a wire goes to D9 in the picture, it goes to the ninth pin along in reality. Drag to orbit, hit Explode to lift the parts apart, turn pin labels on and off.

Soldering is explained in words, joint by joint

Not "solder the header". Which side the plastic goes, which pin to tack first, how long to hold the iron, what the joint should look like when it is right, and what it looks like when it is cold and needs redoing.

The code is complete and copyable

No fragments, no "..." where the interesting bit was. Each sketch compiles as printed, has its libraries listed with install instructions, and is commented so you can see which line to change. One button copies the whole thing.
