New boards

New silicon, new boards, and things announced but not yet buyable. Every entry says which of the projects in this book it actually changes - because most of them change nothing, and a news page that will not say so is just advertising.

How to read this page

The badge is the important part

Shipping means you can put money down today and a box turns up. Preorder means money now, board later, on a date the vendor quoted and may miss. Announced means real specs and a real date, but you cannot buy it at any price. Rumour means someone saw something behind glass. Treat it as fiction until it moves up a rung.

New is not better

Most of what is on this page will not improve a single project in this book, and several entries say exactly that. A board that is twice as fast does not help a project that spends its life waiting for a sensor. The Changes a project here filter is the short list worth your attention.
